# Writings by Harav Ben-Zion Hai Meir Uzziel in Hebrew and in English translation

## Hegionei Uzziel — Jewish philosophy

Includes the complete Hebrew text (downloaded from haravuziel.org.il) reformatted, and a work-in-progress English translation

Note on copyright: Harav Uzziel died in 1953, so to the best of my knowledge his
works are in the public domain in Israel and other countries with a life + 70 years copyright law. English translations on this site are licensed with a Creative Commons [CC BY-SA](creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) license.
